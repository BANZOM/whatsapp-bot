# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 8, 2026

## Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how WhatsApp Bot ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our WhatsApp messaging service.

## Information We Collect

- **Phone number:** We collect your phone number solely to send you WhatsApp messages.
- **Message metadata:** We may receive delivery receipts and read status from the WhatsApp Business API.

We do **not** collect, store, or process:
- Message content beyond what is necessary for delivery
- Personal data such as name, email, or location
- Any data from your WhatsApp profile

## How We Use Your Information

Your phone number is used exclusively to:
- Send messages you have requested or consented to receive
- Respond to your inquiries

## Data Storage and Security

- We do not store message content or personal data in any database.
- Access tokens and credentials are stored securely and never shared publicly.

## Third-Party Services

We use the [Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/whatsapp/cloud-api) to send messages. Your data is processed in accordance with [Meta's Privacy Policy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

## Data Retention

We do not retain your personal data. Phone numbers are used only at the time of message delivery.

## Your Rights

You may:
- Request to stop receiving messages at any time
- Ask us to delete any data we may hold about you

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at:

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted in this repository.
